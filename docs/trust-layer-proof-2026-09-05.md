## 2. FAR 25.341 Discrete Gust Formula

The incremental gust load factor (limit) is:

\[
\Delta n = \frac{K_g \, U_{de} \, V \, C_{L_\alpha}}{ \frac{2W}{S} }
\]

where:

- \( V \) = equivalent airspeed (ft/s) at VC.  
  For a typical transport, VC ≈ 0.85–0.9 \( V_{MO} \). But since not given, we must assume a typical VC.  
  However, the formula is often written in terms of **true airspeed** and **density ratio**.  
  The FAR formula uses:

\[
\Delta n = \frac{K_g \, U_{de} \, V_e \, C_{L_\alpha}}{ \frac{2W}{S} }
\]

where \( V_e \) is **equivalent airspeed** (EAS) in ft/s.  
At sea level, EAS = TAS.
