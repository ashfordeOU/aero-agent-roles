## 2. FAR 25.341 Discrete-Gust Formula

The limit load factor due to a discrete gust is:

\[
n = 1 + \frac{K_g \, U_{de} \, V \, C_{L_\alpha}}{498 \, (W/S)}
\]

- \( U_{de} \) = derived gust velocity (ft/s EAS) at VC

  Let’s use the standard:  
  \( U_{de} = 56 \, \text{ft/s} \) at VC.
